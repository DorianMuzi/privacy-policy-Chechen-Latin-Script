# Chechen Latin Script

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last updated:** November 2026

## 📌 Description

The **Chechen Latin Script** project, a subproject of LinguaCE, is a browser extension (Chrome and Firefox) that transliterates Chechen text written in Cyrillic script into the 1992 Latin alphabet, on the web pages you visit and in its own window. The goal is to make Chechen text easier to access and read in a standardized format.

## ✨ Features

- 🔤 **Transliteration**: converts Chechen Cyrillic text to Latin script directly in your browser (whole page, automatic mode, or a floating bar on the selected text, with a Copy button).
- 🔒 **No data collection**: no personal data is collected, and the extension sends nothing to any server.
- 💾 **Local storage**: your settings and your draft text are saved on your own device.
- 🌐 **Website access**: the extension reads only the text of the pages to convert Chechen Cyrillic, locally.
- 💬 **Optional feedback form**: a "Report" button opens a Google Form (in a new tab) so you can report a wrong transliteration or a bug. Nothing is sent unless you click "Submit" on the form.
- 🗣️ **Interface languages**: Chechen, English, French, Russian, German, Turkish, Spanish, Arabic.

## 🔐 Security and Privacy

- **No personal data is collected.**
- The extension stores your settings and draft text **locally** (`storage.sync` / `storage.local`).
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, ads or trackers.
